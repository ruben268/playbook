Privacy

Shorrt (Ruben Persoons, Ghent) only uses the details you send us to contact you about the playbook pilot. We don't share them with anyone and don't use them for advertising.

Along with your answers, we note whether you used a phone or a computer, which play you viewed last and which link brought you here. This helps us improve the demo.

Your answers are stored in a private Google Sheet that only we can access. We keep them until the pilot ends (summer 2027) and delete them afterwards, unless you become a customer.

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your details at any time: ruben@shorrt.be. If you're not happy with how we handle them, you can contact the Belgian Data Protection Authority (dataprotectionauthority.be).
